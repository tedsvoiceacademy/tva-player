Help

Getting a song in

Drag it into the window from File Explorer, or press Open a song at the bottom left. Add a folder lists everything playable in a folder down the left side for good; nothing is copied or moved.

Play all songs listed below plays the list on the left, top to bottom. If you have typed in the search box it plays what the search left showing.

A playlist is that list kept under a name. Save as a playlist names it; Open a playlist… loads one back and starts playing it.

The four dials

Speed makes the song slower or faster without changing its pitch. Straight up is normal. All the way down is quarter speed, all the way up is double.

Pitch moves the song up or down in half steps without changing its speed.

Pan turns one side of the recording down. Straight up plays both sides.

Volume is how loud the song plays.

Double-click any dial to put it back to normal. You can also click a dial and use the arrow keys.

The three lights by the song name

Playing lights while the song is running. Looping lights while a loop is repeating. Recording lights red while the microphone is recording.

Looping a part

Drag across the wave to mark a loop, or use Set the loop start and Set the loop end, which take whatever time the song is at. You can also type the times, to the tenth of a second, and nudge them with − and +.

Save this loop by name keeps it with the song, so "chorus" is still there next time you open it. Clear the loop only wipes the two times — your saved loops are untouched.

Recording

Recording happens on the player itself, to the right of the playback buttons. Record new (red) records just your voice. Overdub (orange) starts the song and records you against it — overdub is the studio word for singing onto a track that is already there.

Press either one and it records; press the same button again to stop and keep the take. You do not have to turn the microphone on first — it opens by itself. The Mic button turns it on early so you can check your level before you start, and Keep last saves the last two minutes the microphone already heard, so a good run is never lost for want of pressing record.

While the microphone is on, Your voice appears in the display with a bar showing how loud you are. Sing your loudest and keep it out of the red.

Takes are listed under Takes and kept as WAV files on this computer. Save it with the song makes a single file of your take and the song together, for sending to somebody.

The wave

On a stereo song the left channel is drawn on top and the right channel underneath, so you can see which side a part is on. A mono song is drawn as one.

Notes and the click track

A note is pinned to the moment the song is at; click it later to jump there. The click track plays a metronome over the song, with a count-in first.

How it looks

There are ten skins under Set-up — your own brand colours, a light one for a bright room, a plain grey one, a high-contrast one for reading the console across the room, and a few others. Each changes the colours and the finish together, and your choice follows you to your other computer.

Where your things are kept

Loops, notes and playlists follow you between computers through OneDrive. Takes stay on the computer that made them. The exact folders are printed under Set-up.
